SmartBudget Manager
A ready-to-use Excel workbook (SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx) to record your monthly income and
expenses, see where your money goes, and get a warning when you spend more than your budget.
What it does
• Records income and expenses in one table
• Sorts expenses into five categories: Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education
• Calculates total income, total expenses and your monthly balance automatically
• Shows a pie chart of expenses by category
• Warns you when the budget is exceeded: the status turns red and reads OVER BUDGET
What you need
• Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or 365 (desktop version)
• The file SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx
• Nothing else: no installation, no macros, no internet connection
Get started
1. Copy SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx to your computer and open it. If a yellow bar appears, click Enable
Editing.
2. Open the Categories sheet and set the Monthly Budget for each category.
3. Open the Transactions sheet. In the first empty row, enter the Date, Description, Type (Income or
Expense) and Category, then the amount in the Income or Expense column.
4. Open the Dashboard sheet to read your totals, your budget status and the chart.
5. Press Ctrl+S to save. Use a new copy of the file for each month.
The three worksheets
Worksheet What it is for Do you type here?
Dashboard Totals, balance, budget status, summary by

Transactions Your income and expenses (rows 4 to 50) Yes, except the Month column

Good to know
• Use the workbook for one month at a time: the totals add up every transaction in the table.
• The table holds up to 47 transactions.
• Do not type in the Month column or on the Dashboard: they contain formulas.
• Only the five listed categories appear in the Dashboard summary and the chart.
